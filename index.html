Four things to chew on this week
It's Nice That Editorial · Essay · ~7 min

The View From Cairo: on minimalist aesthetics and Arab design

A sharp critique of how "global minimalism" tends to flatten Arab visual culture into a handful of safe, exportable gestures, written from inside Cairo's design scene rather than about it. Essential before any brief that asks for "modern Middle Eastern" — the piece names exactly what that shorthand erases.

Debbie Millman Design Matters · Podcast · ~75 min

Ann Patchett on creativity, marriage, and looking for hope

Recorded live at TED, novelist and bookshop owner Ann Patchett talks with Debbie Millman about sustaining a creative practice across decades and what she's learned from running a business alongside writing one. Not design-specific, but a useful recalibration for anyone whose "creative life" has started to feel like only client work.

It's Nice That Editorial · Interview · ~6 min

Inside OnePlus's quiet cultural excavation of its own brand

A look at how OnePlus's in-house design team is digging back through the brand's own visual history to find what's actually distinctive, rather than chasing the next trend cycle. A good case for any client audit that starts with "what do we already own" instead of "what's everyone else doing."

Sicko: a new book makes a case for airline sick bags as design

A look at a new book-length survey of airline sick-bag design — a genuinely unloved object category treated with the same seriousness as a type specimen. The best argument this month for why "ugly" or overlooked ephemera deserves a design history too.
