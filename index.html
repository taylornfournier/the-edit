No. 01Brand Identity

Templo codes climate in hobo hieroglyphics

Templo for Casi

A handcrafted system of pictograms inspired by Depression-era hobo signs, swapping sustainability's usual alarmism for warmth and pragmatism. For non-profit and ESG-leaning clients drowning in green gradients and stock leaves, this is proof that humanist mark-making out-performs polish — a direction worth pitching to any cause-driven board.
